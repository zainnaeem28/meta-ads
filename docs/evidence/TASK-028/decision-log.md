| 2 | Gap G-1 (plain queue row) | `jobs:migrate` runs `REVOKE INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE ON pgboss.queue FROM app_api` and `REVOKE DELETE ON pgboss.queue FROM app_worker` after the queues exist. `app_worker` keeps INSERT because pg-boss's scheduler inserts its own `__pgboss__send-it` queue row at every start. The runtime wrapper's type has no `createQueue`/`updateQueue`/`deleteQueue` | TASK-025 decision 4 assigned this REVOKE to `jobs:migrate`.
